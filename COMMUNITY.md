# Community principles

World Field Guide aims to make GeoGuessr knowledge more open, useful, and evidence-led.

1. **Evidence over confidence.** Explain what was observed, where, and how often. Confidence is earned, not asserted.
2. **Scope matters.** A clue may apply to one province, road type, or imagery generation—not an entire country.
3. **Challenge ideas, respect people.** Disagreement is welcome when it is specific, sourced, and constructive.
4. **Credit creators.** Do not redistribute images without permission. A link to public imagery is not a redistribution licence.
5. **Make knowledge accessible.** Contributors may submit in any language. The project is currently English-first; translation and clarification can be collaborative.
6. **No gatekeeping by skill.** Beginners can contribute observations. Review determines the status of a claim, not the contributor's rank.
7. **No fabricated certainty.** Keep hypotheses and incomplete observations marked as Draft or In review.
8. **Protect the project.** Spam, harassment, plagiarism, and deliberately misleading submissions may be removed.

## How decisions are made

Contributions are reviewed for geographic scope, repeatability, evidence quality, exceptions, and image rights. Reviewers may request changes, narrow a claim, or decline it. A submission is not automatically published or Verified.
