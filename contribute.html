The goal is not to collect 100,000 random “tips”. It is to preserve 100,000 useful visual examples that can survive review, exceptions, and comparison.

What counts as a contribution?

✓ A visual example

A location showing a specific road, pole, plate, building, landscape, coverage feature, or regional pattern.

✓ Context

Where it appears, what it supports, and what it should not be confused with.

✓ Evidence

A source, repeat observations, or enough examples for reviewers to test the claim.

✕ Not just a vibe

“This feels Brazilian” can inspire a submission, but it cannot be published as a verified clue without evidence.
